ADB Optimizer connects to Android devices over ADB (wireless pairing or a
local connection) or through Shizuku, and gives you a single place to inspect,
tune and clean them up.

Features:
* Device dashboard with CPU, RAM, storage and thermal state.
* Optimization recipes from a catalog, applied step by step and recorded in a
  change journal.
* App management: inventory, bulk actions, debloat lists and protected
  packages.
* Screen mirroring through an embedded scrcpy server.
* Termux integration to run shell commands and Linux recipes.
* TV support: Leanback launcher entry and remote-friendly UI.

Requirements:
* A device with wireless ADB debugging enabled, or Shizuku installed and
  running. The Termux integration needs Termux.
* No root access is required.

Privacy: the app contains no ads, analytics or trackers. The debloat package
list is downloaded from the Universal Android Debloater project only when you
ask it to refresh; everything else stays on the device.
